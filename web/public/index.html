How it works

From photo to logged meal in seconds

No searching food databases or weighing everything. Take a picture, fix anything that looks off, and save.

  1. Snap

    Take a photo of your plate, or pick one from your gallery if you forgot earlier.

  2. AI estimates

    Your chosen model identifies each food and estimates portions, carbs, protein, fat and calories.

  3. You review

    Adjust portions in a tap. The meal type is picked from your local time, and you can change it.

  4. Saved

    The meal is written to Health Connect, so it appears in Google Health, Fitbit and other apps you allow.

Privacy by design

We never see your data, because we don't have servers

Neutrino has no backend, no accounts and no analytics. Your phone talks directly to the AI provider you picked and to your own Health Connect.

  • Your key stays on your phoneEncrypted with Android Keystore. Never sent to us.
  • No tracking, ads or crash reportingNothing phones home. Read the code to check.
  • Backups belong to youEncrypted on your device, stored locally or in your own Google Drive.

Features

Small app, carefully built

Everything you need to log meals fast, and nothing that gets in your way.

Bring your own key

Paste a Gemini or OpenAI API key. You pay the provider directly, usually a fraction of a cent per photo.

Pick your model

Neutrino lists the vision-capable models your key can use, so you can trade speed, cost and accuracy.

Knows what meal it is

Breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack, chosen from your time zone and your own meal windows.

Understands local food

Tell it your cuisine, like Bangladeshi, Indian or Mexican, for better recognition of bhaat, dal, biryani and more.

Water too

Log a glass in one tap. Hydration goes to Health Connect alongside your meals.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Neutrino free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under GPL-3.0. The only cost is what your AI provider charges for your API usage, usually a fraction of a cent per photo, billed to your own account.

Which AI providers are supported?

Google Gemini and OpenAI at launch. Providers are plug-ins, so more can be added by the community.

Do you see my photos or health data?

No. Neutrino has no servers. Photos go straight from your phone to the AI provider you chose, under your account and their terms. Nutrition is saved to Health Connect on your device.

Some providers' free tiers may use submitted content to improve their services. Check your provider's terms if that matters to you.

How accurate are the estimates?

Photo-based estimates are approximate. Portion size is the hardest part to judge from a picture. That's why Neutrino always shows you the numbers to review before saving. Neutrino is not a medical device. Don't use it as the sole basis for medical decisions such as insulin dosing.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Neutrino is built for Android and Health Connect. Android 14 and newer include Health Connect; on older versions you can install it from Google Play.

What happens if I uninstall or clear the app's data?

Meals you've already saved stay in Health Connect. Your settings, history and API key can be restored from your encrypted local backup or your Google Drive backup.

What can Neutrino access in my Google Drive?

Only a private, hidden folder that belongs to Neutrino. It cannot see or change any of your other files, and you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
